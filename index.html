01Proprietary platform · live on MT5

Private Trading Research & Execution Platform

Learns from every trade: its decision policies are trained on live outcomes, and a challenger replaces the champion only after it proves better out of sample.

The problem
Most trading ideas are never tested against real costs, real risk limits and a real broker before money is on the line.
What it does
It takes a market idea from data to research, backtesting, risk checks and live orders on MT5, then checks the results against the broker's records.

Proprietary · built end to end

02Proprietary product · intraday forecasting, live on MT5

Intraday Forecasting & Trading Co-pilot

Intraday forecasting for trading: the trader sets the limits; the co-pilot forecasts the day, proposes the trade and executes inside them.

The problem
Intraday, a trader has to read how the day is unfolding and act fast; one wrong size or price in a manual order can cost real money.
What it does
An intraday forecasting engine tracks the state of the trading day, updates the next price targets and classifies how the day is likely to end, recalibrated nightly from 6,726 historical sessions. A macro news outlook frames swing decisions. Orders go to MetaTrader 5 with trader confirmation or through a bounded autopilot inside limits the trader sets.

Proprietary · built end to end

59orders published to MetaTrader 5 since 3 Nov 2026: 36 confirmed by the trader and 23 sent by a bounded autopilot inside limits the trader arms per symbol (risk, reward-to-risk, stop time, max attempts).

54,763records in a hash-chained journal; uncertain broker submissions go to reconciliation instead of being retried blindly.

6,726historical sessions recalibrate the forecasts every night.

03Proprietary platform · Research & Development, Oxford, UK (Sep - Oct 2026)

Energy Procurement Research & Backtesting Platform

The problem
I worked on-site in Oxford, UK, for a large energy buyer that spends heavily on power and gas every year and needed to lower its purchase cost.
What it does
A research and backtesting platform for power and gas procurement in European markets. It turns each buying idea into a testable rule, replays it on real exchange order-book and trade data with costs, and compares the result with the buyer's current practice and a market benchmark.

Proprietary · built end to end

2.5%below the buyer's current practice in 3 of 3 quarters (fees excluded), in its first exploratory backtest.

Built in under a month. First applied on-site in Oxford, UK (Sep - Oct 2026), to a large energy buyer's procurement.

04Proprietary platform · AI coding agents

Agentic Workflow Orchestration Platform (AWOP)

Agentic workflow orchestration: AI agents ship code, independent agents review it, tests decide what merges.

Multi-agent orchestration and coordination: agents share task ownership, handoffs and review rules. MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects assistants to the workflow; evals measure runs by model and role. Self-healing mechanisms handle stalled work and failed handoffs within explicit limits.

The problem
AI coding agents write code fast, but without structure nobody can say what changed, whether it was tested or who checked it.
What it does
It coordinates several AI agents in parallel, each on its own branch, with test gates on every merge and independent review on every autopilot merge. Human decisions and run records stay part of the workflow.

Proprietary · built end to end

341tasks merged across the four projects by AI agents, every one through the test gate.

It develops the trading decision engine and the trading co-pilot today, and it built the energy platform.

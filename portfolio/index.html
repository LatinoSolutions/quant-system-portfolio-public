01Proprietary platform · live on MT5

Private Trading Research & Execution Platform

The problem
Most trading ideas are never tested against real costs, real risk limits and a real broker before money is on the line.
What it does
It takes a market idea from data to research, backtesting, risk checks and live orders on MT5, then checks the results against the broker's records.

Proprietary · built end to end

1,217order records from live execution on MT5, with profit and loss reconciled against the broker to the cent.

This describes the engineering, never the strategy.

Key terms, in plain words
Execution-aware backtesting
Replaying past markets to see what a rule would have done, including orders that would not have filled and the broker's costs.
Monte Carlo stress testing
Thousands of reshuffled histories, to see how bad it can get before any money is at risk.
Position sizing and drawdown limits
How big each trade may be, and a hard cap on how much the account may lose in a day and in total.
Broker reconciliation
Internal records matched against the broker's, down to the cent.
Systematic Trading Decision Engine
The next layer: a candidate decision rule must beat simpler baselines and pass statistical gates, under a safety veto that can always block it, before it can reach capital.
Silent-failure detection
A channel that is running but no longer writing is caught, instead of being reported as a normal day.

08 · NEXT
Systematic Trading Decision Engine
Offline policy learning · Off-policy evaluation · Champion-challenger governance

The platform is evolving from deterministic rules into a decision engine above the chain. The data picks the model family, with reinforcement learning among the candidates. Every candidate policy must beat simpler baselines and pass statistical promotion gates under a hard safety veto before it can reach capital. In plain words: a new decision rule is learned from recorded history and judged on past data before it risks money (offline policy learning, off-policy evaluation), and it replaces the rule in use only if it beats it (champion-challenger). The foundation so far: 138 TypeScript modules, 27 typed contract modules and 30 JSON schemas, verified by a validation suite of 6,340 passing tests, none failing, with 125 of 139 planned tasks delivered.

The Private Trading Research & Execution Platform is proprietary. This page describes the scope of the engineering, not the strategy logic.

02Proprietary product · voice execution on MT5

Trading Execution Copilot

The problem
When a trader places orders by hand under time pressure, one wrong size or price can cost real money.
What it does
The trader says the order out loud. The copilot turns it into a ticket, checks it, sizes the risk, reads it back and sends it to MT5 only after an explicit yes, then reconciles it with the broker.

Proprietary · built end to end

Nothing reaches MT5 without the trader's explicit yes, and every order is read back first.

Key terms, in plain words
Discretionary execution
The trader decides the trade; the copilot handles the order safely.
Ticket
The spoken order, written down as a structured order that software can check.
Risk sizing
The position size is worked out from the risk limit, not typed by hand.
Read back
The order is repeated back to the trader before anything is sent.
Broker reconciliation
An order counts as executed only when the broker's own record confirms it.
Evidence for technical readers
  • The path from spoken order to broker is built as separate, tested parts: a dictated-order parser, a ticket validator, a lot calculator and the read-back.
  • Nothing is sent without explicit confirmation, and nothing counts as executed without a reconciled broker acknowledgement.
  • 776 automated tests passing, none failing.
  • Built with the agent platform, AWOP (system 04).

03Proprietary platform · European power and gas

Energy Procurement Research & Backtesting Platform

The problem
A large energy buyer spends heavily on power and gas every year and needed to lower its purchase cost.
What it does
A research and backtesting platform for power and gas procurement in European markets. It turns each buying idea into a testable rule, replays it on real exchange order-book and trade data with costs, and compares the result with the buyer's current practice and a market benchmark.

Proprietary · built end to end

2.5%below the buyer's current practice in 3 of 3 quarters (fees excluded), in its first exploratory backtest.

Built in under a month, for monthly and quarterly purchases. First applied on-site in Oxford, UK (Sep - Oct 2026), to a large energy buyer's procurement.

The first 5 buying strategies are ready for validation, and the method is open-ended: new strategies and hypotheses can be added for each buying problem to find the best result. An out-of-sample period stays sealed until a rule is frozen.

Data and detailed results stay confidential.

Key terms, in plain words
Energy procurement
How a company buys the power and gas it needs, here in monthly and quarterly products.
Hypothesis-driven research
Each idea is written as a testable claim, with its acceptance criteria, before it is measured.
Backtest and replay
Running a buying rule over past market data, with trading costs, as if it had been used at the time.
Point-in-time data
Each simulated decision sees only what was known at that moment.
Out-of-sample period
Data set aside before any selection, so the result is not tuned to it.
Market benchmark
A market reference to compare against, so a result means something beyond itself.

04Proprietary platform · AI coding agents

Agentic Workflow Orchestration Platform (AWOP)

Multi-agent orchestration and coordination: agents share task ownership, handoffs and review rules. MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects assistants to the workflow; evals measure runs by model and role. Self-healing mechanisms handle stalled work and failed handoffs within explicit limits.

The problem
AI coding agents write code fast, but without structure nobody can say what changed, whether it was tested or who checked it.
What it does
It coordinates several AI agents in parallel, each on its own branch, with test gates on every merge and independent review on every autopilot merge. Human decisions and run records stay part of the workflow.

Proprietary · built end to end

272tasks merged across the four projects by AI agents, every one through the test gate.

It develops the trading decision engine and execution copilot today, and it built the energy platform. The direction is an Augmented Intelligence Workspace: AI extends human judgement through coordinated reasoning and action.

Key terms, in plain words
Multi-agent orchestration
Several AI agents working on the same codebase at once, each on its own task.
Multi-agent coordination
Shared task ownership, handoffs, evidence and review responsibilities across agents and model families.
Augmented intelligence
AI supports human judgement. The direction is a workspace for coordinated reasoning and action, with the person retaining authority over goals, risk and approvals.
Self-healing workflows
Recovery mechanisms detect stalled work, stop repeated ineffective corrections and route the next action. Recovery does not waive budgets, human decisions or independent verification, and does not guarantee that every failure resolves without intervention.
MCP (Model Context Protocol)
A standard connector that lets an outside AI assistant see and operate the platform, through the same interface a person uses.
Evals
Every agent run is measured by model and role, so the choice of model rests on numbers, not impressions.
Model routing and failover
Each kind of job goes to a chosen account and model; when an account runs low on quota, the work moves to another one.
Isolated branch
Each agent writes in its own copy of the code, so parallel work cannot collide.
Test-regression gate
Nothing merges if the tests get worse than main.
Independent review
A different agent, not the one that wrote the change, checks the exact diff.
Autopilot merge
On routes agreed in advance, a change merges without a person once it has passed the gate and the independent review.
First-pass approval and rework rate
How often a change is accepted at once, and how often it goes back to be redone.

01Proprietary platform · live on MT5

Private Trading Research & Execution Platform

The problem
Most trading ideas are never tested against real costs, real risk limits and a real broker before money is on the line.
What it does
It takes a market idea from data to research, backtesting, risk checks and live orders on MT5, then checks the results against the broker's records.

Proprietary · built end to end

1,217order records from live execution on MT5, with profit and loss reconciled against the broker to the cent.

This describes the engineering, never the strategy.

02Proprietary product · voice execution on MT5

Trading Execution Copilot

The problem
When a trader places orders by hand under time pressure, one wrong size or price can cost real money.
What it does
The trader says the order out loud. The copilot turns it into a ticket, checks it, sizes the risk, reads it back and sends it to MT5 only after an explicit yes, then reconciles it with the broker.

Proprietary · built end to end

Nothing reaches MT5 without the trader's explicit yes, and every order is read back first.

03Proprietary platform · European power and gas

Energy Procurement Research & Backtesting Platform

The problem
A large energy buyer spends heavily on power and gas every year and needed to lower its purchase cost.
What it does
A research and backtesting platform for power and gas procurement in European markets. It turns each buying idea into a testable rule, replays it on real exchange order-book and trade data with costs, and compares the result with the buyer's current practice and a market benchmark.

Proprietary · built end to end

2.5%below the buyer's current practice in 3 of 3 quarters (fees excluded), in its first exploratory backtest.

Built in under a month. First applied on-site in Oxford, UK (Sep - Oct 2026), to a large energy buyer's procurement.

Data and detailed results stay confidential.

04Proprietary platform · AI coding agents

Agentic Workflow Orchestration Platform (AWOP)

Multi-agent orchestration and coordination: agents share task ownership, handoffs and review rules. MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects assistants to the workflow; evals measure runs by model and role. Self-healing mechanisms handle stalled work and failed handoffs within explicit limits.

The problem
AI coding agents write code fast, but without structure nobody can say what changed, whether it was tested or who checked it.
What it does
It coordinates several AI agents in parallel, each on its own branch, with test gates on every merge and independent review on every autopilot merge. Human decisions and run records stay part of the workflow.

Proprietary · built end to end

272tasks merged across the four projects by AI agents, every one through the test gate.

It develops the trading decision engine and execution copilot today, and it built the energy platform. The direction is an Augmented Intelligence Workspace: AI extends human judgement through coordinated reasoning and action.

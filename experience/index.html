Energy Procurement Research & Backtesting Platform · proprietary platform

A research and backtesting platform for power and gas procurement in European markets, built for monthly and quarterly purchases. Built in under a month. In its first exploratory backtest, the quarterly power buying rule came in 2.5% below the buyer's current practice in 3 of 3 delivery quarters (fees excluded), inside the 1 to 5% range McKinsey cites for strong energy procurement. The first 5 buying strategies are ready for validation, and the method is open-ended: new strategies and hypotheses can be added for each buying problem to find the best result. An out-of-sample period stays sealed until a rule is frozen. How it works and the evidence.
